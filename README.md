# TunesPlayer
A music player that has a small library of songs that I like!
![image of the music player](image.png)
## Features

4 Songs! Such as:

- HEAVEN SAYS by chart
- The Player on The Other Side by Honkai: Star Rail 2.0 OST
- On The Moon by Chromaa ft. MIGURIMA
- Armed and Ready by: Jeff Williams ft. Casey Lee Williams

### Settings:

- Skip songs
- Volume control
- Add/remove songs on a playlist

## Why was this made?

This was made for the Pixl Hack Club program, for the Music Player trial!
This was nice in learning more about javascript and learning how to better format a webpage.

## AI Usage

I used Claude to help me debug and to figure out why some things weren't working as intended, such as my playlist skip order not going in the right order, or my remove button not working.
